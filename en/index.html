Process

How we work

  1. I

    Brief

    A short call or questionnaire: goals, audience, timing. We agree the scope and price.

  2. II

    Concept

    Page structure and direction: grid, typography, mood. Agreed before the details.

  3. III

    Design

    Every block on desktop and mobile. I present, we revise, we sign off.

  4. IV

    Development

    Built in code or on Tilda, with animation and responsive layout. Tested on phones and across browsers.

  5. V

    Launch

    Publishing, domain and analytics — if they are in scope. I hand over all access.
