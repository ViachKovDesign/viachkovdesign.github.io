Privacy policy

Updated 4 November 2026

Who is responsible for the site

The data controller is Viacheslav Kovalenko, a self-employed web designer and developer (registered under Russia’s professional income tax regime). He runs viachkovdesign.github.io and is responsible for the data processing described below.

For any questions about your data, email the address appears when JavaScript is on or message me on Telegram at @viachkovdesign.

What data is collected and why

The site has no forms, sign-up or payments. I don’t ask for your name, phone number or email and don’t store them. If you write to me on Telegram or by email, I see only what you send and use it only to reply and work on your project.

The only third-party service is the Yandex Metrica counter. It loads after the page has loaded, as the notice at the bottom of the screen explains. Metrica receives anonymised information about the visit: pages and clicks, scrolling, device and browser type, screen size, approximate region based on IP address, and the referral source. Session replay (recording of mouse movement and scrolling) is turned off.

There is one purpose — site analytics: to understand which sections people read and where they drop off, so the site can be improved. This information cannot identify a specific person without additional data, which I don’t have.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a site leaves in your browser. This site uses the following:

NamePurpose
_ym_uid, _ym_dVisitor ID and date of the first visit. Kept for up to 1 year.
_ym_isadWhether the visitor sees ads. Kept for up to 2 days.
vk-noticeMarks that you closed the cookie notice. Stored in your browser (localStorage) and never sent anywhere.
vk-ldMarks that the intro has already been shown during this visit (sessionStorage). Never sent anywhere.

Retention periods follow Yandex’s documentation and may change on their side. Fonts and images are served from this site’s own address; there are no advertising or social media cookies.

Who the data is shared with

Counter data is processed by Yandex LLC under its own policies and stored on Yandex servers. Site data is not shared with anyone else. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub sees technical server requests (such as IP addresses), like any hosting provider.

How to opt out and delete data

  • You can stop data collection in your browser: block cookies for this site or use a tracker blocker. The site will keep working.
  • You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.
  • To ask me to delete data about your visit or our correspondence, email or message me on Telegram. Metrica data is linked to the cookie ID, so please include its value (_ym_uid) — otherwise your visit can’t be found.

Changes

If the data or services change, I will update this page and the date at the top.
